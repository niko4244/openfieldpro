# Code of Conduct

OpenFieldPro is committed to a respectful, harassment-free project community.

Be considerate, discuss technical disagreements in good faith, respect privacy, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and made without regard to contributor or sponsor status. Sponsorship never purchases exceptions to this policy.
