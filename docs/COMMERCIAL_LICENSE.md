# Commercial licensing (placeholder)

OpenFieldPro is AGPL-3.0. For most users — including businesses self-hosting
it for their own field-service operations — the AGPL costs nothing and
requires nothing beyond what you're already doing.

You may want a commercial license/exception if you:

- offer a **modified** OpenFieldPro as a hosted service and cannot publish

- need to embed OpenFieldPro in a proprietary product.

The copyright holder retains the right to grant such exceptions. Terms are
negotiated case-by-case; contact the maintainer through the repository.
